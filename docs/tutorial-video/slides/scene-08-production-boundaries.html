Production Boundaries

The sink is intentionally focused: typed ingestion and transient reliability.

Inside the Carbon ClickHouse sink

  • Typed row conversion from generated decoders
  • Generated landing schemas and DDL
  • Schema bootstrap and safe reconciliation
  • Local buffering, flushing, retries, and backoff
  • Insert settings, dedup tokens, and sink metrics
The sink writes append-only landing data. It does not own product-serving semantics.

Outside the sink

  • Durable queues and replay orchestration
  • Source checkpoints and slot coverage policy
  • Dead-letter queues and operator workflows
  • Finality, reorg, and reprocessing policy
  • Serving tables, materialized views, and APIs
ClickHouse handles storage topology, compression, merges, and analytical query serving.
